Credit price calculator

Drag a widget's price and watch the gross margin move on all three plans at once. Costs are the live fal rate for the endpoint each widget actually calls. Your edits are saved in this browser.

Margin is gross margin on revenue — (revenue − fal cost) / revenue, per generation. The three plans grant credits at different prices, so one credit price produces three different margins: 12 Month $0.000521/cr, Monthly $0.001874/cr, Weekly $0.002995/cr.

The ticks under each slider mark where that plan crosses zero. A price left of a tick loses money on that plan.

Costs read from the live fal catalog and model pages on 1 September 2026; credit prices from the production persona endpoint. Plans: 12 Month $49.99/yr & 8,000 cr/mo · Monthly $14.99/mo & 8,000 cr/mo · Weekly $5.99/wk & 2,000 cr/wk.
